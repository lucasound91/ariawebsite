The ARIA experience

Where strings meet electronic sound.

A meeting of organic expression and electronic rhythm. ARIA brings live violin improvisation into the world of DJ performance, creating a sound that is immersive, unexpected and entirely in the moment.

Moving between atmospheric melodies, hypnotic rhythms and uplifting energy, each performance unfolds as a unique musical journey.

More than music

The artist

Rooted in music. Inspired by possibility.

ARIA is a South African violinist and DJ whose musical journey brings together classical foundations, electronic music and a love of live performance.

With a background in classical violin and a passion for exploring sound, rhythm and improvisation, she creates performances that move beyond traditional musical boundaries.

Based in Cape Town, ARIA performs for weddings, events and unique destinations across South Africa and beyond.
