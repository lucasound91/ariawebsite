The art of atmosphere

The invisible design element.

We carefully consider the spaces and experiences we create for people. The architecture, lighting, textures, scents, food and natural surroundings all contribute to how a place makes us feel.

Yet music is often an afterthought.

Music is an incredibly powerful, yet often overlooked, element of experiential design. It has the ability to shift our emotional state, invite presence, encourage connection and transform the atmosphere of a space.

ARIA Atmosphere brings intention to the music within these environments.

Whether creating a sense of stillness during meditation, warmth around a shared table, or a feeling of openness during a sunset experience, music becomes an integral part of the experience rather than simply background entertainment.

The music doesn't need to be the main act. It provides the foundation for everything else to unfold.
How we work

Music, thoughtfully curated for the moment.

Every space has its own character. Every experience has its own energy. The music should reflect and enhance both.

ARIA Atmosphere works across two interconnected environments:

01

Setting the tone for spaces

Creating atmosphere through music.

Thoughtfully curated music that establishes the character and atmosphere of a physical environment.

From calming ambient soundscapes to warm, uplifting electronic music, each musical selection is considered in relation to the space, its surroundings and the people within it.

Whether creating a welcoming arrival experience, an inviting dining atmosphere or a sense of tranquillity in a wellness space, music becomes an intentional part of the environment.

Ideal for
  • Boutique hotels and lodges
  • Restaurants and dining spaces
  • Spas and wellness facilities
  • Reception and relaxation areas
  • Hospitality and lifestyle spaces
02

Setting the tone for experiences

Music designed around the journey.

Music thoughtfully curated around the intention, energy and emotional progression of a particular experience.

From gentle ambient soundscapes for breathwork and meditation to evolving musical journeys for movement, reflection and connection, each selection is designed to complement the activity and the experience being created.

Music can help establish a sense of calm, encourage emotional openness, inspire movement or create space for deeper connection.

Ideal for
  • Yoga and meditation
  • Breathwork and movement
  • Journaling and reflection
  • Wellness retreats and workshops
  • Ceremonies and immersive experiences
  • Conscious gatherings and events
The formats

Different ways to bring music to life.

Whether you're looking for a carefully curated musical environment or an immersive live experience, ARIA Atmosphere offers two ways to bring music into your space or offering.

01

Playlist curation

Bespoke musical collections for spaces and experiences.

Carefully selected and sequenced music, curated around the atmosphere of a venue or the intention of a particular experience.

Drawing on decades of musical creation and curation, each collection considers mood, texture, rhythm, instrumentation and emotional progression.

From establishing a distinctive musical identity for a boutique hotel to creating a soundtrack for a 60-minute breathwork session, every playlist is thoughtfully considered.

A soundtrack shaped around the feeling you want to create.
02

Live experiences

An immersive expression of music.

For moments that call for something more, ARIA brings together expressive live violin, electronic music and atmospheric soundscapes to create immersive musical experiences.

Drawing on ambient textures, organic and melodic electronic music, and improvisational expression, each performance is shaped around the energy of the moment.

Live instrumentation may include violin, piano and sound bowls, depending on the nature and intention of the experience.

From intimate meditation sessions and retreat ceremonies to sunset gatherings and dining experiences, live music adds another dimension to the atmosphere.

Music that becomes part of the experience itself.
THE FEELING BEHIND THE MUSIC

Three expressions.
One atmosphere.

Every space and experience carries its own energy. Music can meet that energy in different ways — creating room for stillness, movement or connection.

STILL

Quietening · Meditative · Introspective

An invitation to slow down, turn inward and become present. Atmospheric soundscapes, gentle textures and meditative compositions create space for stillness, reflection and connection with oneself.

FLOW

Expressive · Energising · Heart-opening

A journey of movement, openness and emotional connection. Evolving soundscapes, warm organic rhythms, melodic electronic music and expressive instrumentation create a sense of flow and possibility.

GATHER

Uplifting · Warm · Connecting

Music that brings people together and celebrates the moment. Warm, uplifting sounds and expressive live performance create an inviting atmosphere for shared experiences, connection and appreciation of our surroundings.
